# SEO audit and proposed copy (English pages) - APPLIED locally, not deployed yet

Source: live smallpdf.us on 2026-11-21. Titles shown without the " | SmallPDF.us" suffix. Em dashes are removed in every proposed line.

## Problems found
1. Homepage H1 "PDF tools that actually just work" has no keyword. Homepage title/description are generic ("Free PDF Tools: Convert, Compress & Merge").
2. /excel-to-pdf H1 is built from the wrong keys: "Upload Your Excel Spreadsheet Free Online Excel to PDF Converter".
3. /ocr-pdf description says "supports 100+ languages". That is false (English, Vietnamese, Arabic, Turkish only).
4. Many H1s are bare names ("Rotate PDF", "Protect PDF", "Redact PDF", "Edit PDF", "Compress Images", "Merge PDF Files") with no search phrase.
5. Several descriptions are short (under 110 characters) and carry no long-tail phrase: word-to-pdf (99), unlock-pdf (101), pdf-to-jpg, pdf-to-ppt, jpg-to-pdf.
6. Mixed separators in titles (— – - |) and em dashes in 12+ titles/descriptions.
7. /pricing title is only "Pricing"; about/contact/terms/privacy are brand-only (fine, they are not ranking pages).

## Em dash removal (scope)
- Messages: en 500, vi 500, id 426, pt 420, fr 404, de 392, it 361, es 340, ja 308, hi 206, ar 76, tr 75 (about 4,000 occurrences in 12 locales).
- Page and component code: about 220 visible lines (titles, alt text, placeholders like "—" for empty values) plus 339 comment-only lines that visitors never see.
- public/llms files: 5. Backend: 293 occurrences, mostly comments; user-visible ones (emails, API errors) need a check.
- Blog posts live in the production database and are NOT in the repo; they need a separate DB pass.
- Proposed rule: titles, descriptions and H1s are rewritten by hand (above). Everywhere else " - " becomes " - ", and a lone "—" placeholder becomes "-". Code comments are left alone.
