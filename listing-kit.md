## What NOT to do

- ❌ No Fiverr/paid link packages, no PBNs, no directory-blast tools
- ❌ Don't submit everything the same day — spread over 2 weeks
- ❌ Don't use the exact same description everywhere — the 3 lengths above give natural variation; tweak a word or two per site
